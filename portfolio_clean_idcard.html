Featured Project

Fixing a real UX gap

"People You May Know" — Letterboxd Friend-Discovery Prototype
Independent Project · Python · UX Research

Letterboxd (30M+ users) offers only two ways to find friends on the platform: syncing an X/Twitter or Facebook account, or searching an exact username. No contact sync, no QR code, no algorithmic suggestions, confirmed directly in the company's own FAQ.

"I find Letterboxd pretty boring because I can't find any of my friends on there. They only offer integration with X which I don't use."

I designed and built a Python prototype for a multi-signal friend-suggestion algorithm, combining three signals into one weighted match score:

01

Taste similarity

Jaccard similarity on shared highly-rated films

02

Location proximity

Same-city matching

03

Mutual connections

Overlap in who users already follow

This mirrors how real recommendation systems (LinkedIn, Instagram) blend multiple signals rather than relying on one. In testing, it surfaced strong matches that a taste-only version missed entirely.
